SAMPLE PRIOR-AUTHORIZATION POLICY (SYNTHETIC / TRAINING USE ONLY)
Fictional Payer: "Meridian Health Plan" — this document is written for classroom demo
purposes and does not represent any real insurer's policy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MRI Brain, with and without contrast (CPT 70553)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Required for approval:
  - Clinical notes from the ordering provider documenting presenting symptoms
  - Proof that conservative treatment was attempted and failed, OR
  - A completed red-flag symptom form (e.g. new neurological deficit,
    suspected mass, post-trauma with red flags)
Clinical criteria: at least one red-flag indication must be present, or
conservative treatment must have been tried for a minimum of 4 weeks without
improvement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MRI Lumbar Spine, without contrast (CPT 72148)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Required for approval:
  - Clinical notes documenting duration and severity of symptoms
  - Proof that conservative treatment (physical therapy, medication, or
    both) was attempted for at least 6 weeks without adequate improvement
Clinical criteria: absence of red-flag symptoms shifts this to a
conservative-treatment-first pathway; presence of red flags allows
immediate approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Therapeutic Exercise / Physical Therapy (CPT 97110)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Required for approval:
  - Clinical notes describing functional limitation
  - A written treatment plan with goals and expected duration
  - A baseline functional assessment score
Clinical criteria: functional assessment score must fall below the plan's
threshold for medical necessity; requests beyond the standard visit cap
require additional justification of continued functional deficit.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Respiratory Assist Device (HCPCS E0470)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Required for approval:
  - Clinical notes documenting the respiratory diagnosis
  - A qualifying sleep study report
  - A signed physician order specifying device settings
Clinical criteria: sleep study must show qualifying event index above the
plan's threshold; requests without a qualifying study are referred for
clinical review rather than automatic denial.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Specialty Injectable Drug (HCPCS J3490)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Required for approval:
  - Clinical notes confirming diagnosis and treatment history
  - Recent lab results relevant to the diagnosis
  - Proof of step therapy (that lower-cost alternatives were tried first),
    unless a documented contraindication is on file
Clinical criteria: step therapy must be documented as tried-and-failed, or
a contraindication must be clearly stated by the prescriber.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE FOR AUTOMATION DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two very different failure modes get routed to the same "denied" queue:
  (a) Procedural gaps — one or more required documents were never
      submitted. These are objectively checkable against the required-docs
      list above and safe to flag automatically for provider follow-up.
  (b) Clinical judgment calls — all documents were submitted, but the
      clinical picture does not clearly meet criteria. These require a
      licensed reviewer's judgment and should NOT be auto-denied or
      auto-approved.
